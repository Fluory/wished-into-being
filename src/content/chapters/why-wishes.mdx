Most repositories are made by the people who maintain them. This one is made by the people who visit it.

Anyone with a GitHub account can open a **wish**: one thing that should exist on a small island – a lantern, a
lighthouse, a cat that ignores everyone. Everybody else can vote with a 👍. Every morning at 08:59 a scheduled Claude
routine grants the most-wanted wish that fits, draws it as a tiny sprite, finds it a place on the island and writes one
line of lore about it. Then it makes exactly one commit.

<IslandFigure />

## Why a repository

A repository already has everything a wishing well needs. **Issues** are the wishes, with a form to fill in and
reactions built in. **Commits** are the proof that something happened, in order, forever. The **history** is the
chronicle – you can check out any day and see the island exactly as it was.

Nothing runs in between. There is no server and no database, only [`world/world.json`](https://github.com/Fluory/wished-into-being/blob/main/world/world.json),
a command-line tool that knows the rules, and a routine that uses it once a day.

## Why only one wish a day

If every wish came true at once, a vote would mean nothing. One wish a day makes the thumbs matter: the brightest star
comes true tomorrow, the others keep shining. And it gives the island a rhythm worth coming back for – something new
every morning, never a flood.

<Callout icon="✦">
  If your wish comes true, the logbook says <strong>wished by @you</strong>, the issue closes with a thank-you, and you
  are a co-author of that day’s commit – your avatar is part of the island’s history.
</Callout>

## What the island will not become

The island is small and kind. Wishes for anything hurtful or violent, for politics, real people, brands, logos or
links are declined with one friendly comment. Everything else simply waits its turn – usually for room, because the
island has to grow first. How it grows is in [chapter three](/chapters/the-sea-and-the-rules).

## An archipelago

This is one of three islands, each grown by a different force from the same idea: a routine that makes one commit a
day. [One Tile a Day](https://github.com/Fluory/one-tile-a-day) grows by its own rules, a living world. [Grow](https://github.com/Fluory/Grow)
grows with the real weather in Heilbronn. This one grows from wishes.
