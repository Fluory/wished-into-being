Most generative worlds appear all at once. You press a button and a whole continent unfolds in a second. **One Tile a Day** does the opposite: the island is only allowed to change once per day, and only by a single tile.

## A commit as a unit of time

The island lives in a public GitHub repository. Its entire state is one file, [`world/world.json`](https://github.com/Fluory/one-tile-a-day/blob/main/world/world.json), and every day exactly one commit changes it. The commit history is not a side effect – it *is* the story:

```text
Day 3: Grass spreads on the western dunes
Day 2: Land rises off the north-west coast
Day 1: Land rises off the north-east coast
Day 0: A sandbank in the open sea
```

Because the map inside `world.json` is stored as rows of characters, a daily diff is literally one character that changed – `~` becomes `.` when a strip of sand rises from the sea.

## Slow on purpose

One tile is almost nothing. That is the point. You cannot binge this island; you can only come back. After a week there is a meadow, after a month a first house and someone living in it, after a year a village with a harbour, a lighthouse and a library – every one of those things placed on a specific day, for a reason, with a line of lore that says why.

<Callout icon="🕰️">
  The routine runs at **08:47 in Heilbronn** – every day, forever. If it runs twice, the second run changes nothing. If it fails, the day stays empty and the logbook shows the gap. There is no catching up.
</Callout>

## Rules instead of randomness

A world that only grows by chance turns into noise. This island follows rules: a house needs land around it, a harbour needs a coast and three houses, the lighthouse needs rock by the sea. The code enforces them – the routine can only choose among legal changes. [Chapter 2](/chapters/world-rules) shows all of them.

## Open by default

Everything that shapes the island is in the repository: the rules, the renderer, the prompt the routine follows ([`ROUTINE.md`](https://github.com/Fluory/one-tile-a-day/blob/main/ROUTINE.md)) and the complete history. The island is transparently **AI-maintained** – Claude writes the lore and makes the daily choice, and every commit says so.

You are welcome to read along, fork it, or start an island of your own.
