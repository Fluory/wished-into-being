Every day the routine asks the same question: *what could happen next?* The answer is never "anything". It is a list of changes that are legal right now, computed by [`rules.ts`](https://github.com/Fluory/one-tile-a-day/blob/main/src/features/world/rules.ts).

## Three kinds of change

- **Ground** – the terrain of one tile changes. Sand rises from the sea, sand turns into meadow, rock breaks through, trees grow into forest.
- **Structures** – something is built on a free tile: a tree, a house, a path, a well, a jetty, a lighthouse …
- **Life** – someone moves into a house, or an animal settles on the ground it likes.

## Everything waits for something

The interesting part are the dependencies. A few examples:

- The very first meadow needs sand on all four sides – grass never grows on the coast.
- A house needs land on three sides and keeps a tile of space to its neighbours. After the first house, new ones stay within three tiles of the village.
- The **library** waits for three houses and wants to stand next to a path.
- The **harbour** needs a sandy coast, three houses and a jetty nearby.
- The **lighthouse** needs rock by the sea – and a house for its keeper.
- **Ruins** only surface far away from every house, once the island is big enough to have forgotten something.

Inhabitants have trades, and trades have conditions too: a keeper only arrives once there is a lighthouse, a farmer needs a field, a miller needs a windmill.

## The director

Claude chooses the daily change, but the island also has a rule-based **director** that can decide on its own. It weighs what the island needs – room to build, people for empty houses, a first harbour once it becomes possible – adds a little variety (no three houses in a row) and draws one option. The director is deterministic: the same island on the same day always gets the same suggestion.

The director is the safety net. Its suggestion is part of the daily plan, and if the routine cannot make a good choice, it lets the director decide. Those days are marked *auto* in the logbook.

<Callout icon="🧪">
  The rules are tested: a simulated year must be valid on every single day, a second change on the same day must fail, and a tampered log must be detected.
</Callout>
