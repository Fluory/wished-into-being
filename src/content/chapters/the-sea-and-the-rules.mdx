The island starts as a well on a round patch of sand. Everything after that is wishes – and the sea.

## Two tiles every dawn

Every morning, **before** the day’s wish, the sea raises two tiles of shore. It prefers tiles with a lot of land
around them, so bays fill up, and a hidden field unique to this island, so it grows capes and inlets instead of a
perfect circle. Land never sinks again.

Grass and sand are not stored as choices: a tile with land on all four sides is grass, every other land tile is beach.
The terrain is a pure function of which tiles are land – so the sea shapes the meadows, too.

## Six kinds, and room for each

A wish is one of six kinds. Each kind has its ground and a number of places that grows with the island:

The limits keep the island varied: if everybody wishes for houses, the houses have to wait while the lanterns, owls
and boats catch up. A wish that does not fit **yet** is not refused – it stays open as a star and gets one comment
saying what is missing.

## Why it never locks up

A rule system like this can paint itself into a corner: if every kind were full and no tile free, nothing could ever
come true again. Here the numbers make that impossible. Every tile of land adds a little room to every kind:

<GrowthNumbers />

Together that is more than one place per tile – and the sea adds two tiles a day while only one wish comes true. So
some kind always has room, and the free land never runs out. The tests grow a whole year of messages in a bottle and
check that every single day found a place.

## Harbours

Boats and fish need water next to the shore. As the island grows, it would slowly swallow them into ponds – so the sea
keeps two tiles of open water around every water wish. The island grows around them and leaves a little harbour.

## Checked from the first day

`npm run world:check` replays the whole log from the well: every dawn has to raise free shore, every wish has to
stand on the right ground, on a free tile, with room for its kind – on the day it came true. Change one character in
`world.json` by hand and the check fails.
